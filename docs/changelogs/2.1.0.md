## News
- **Make your own collections in game**
> Operators (level 2) now have the creator: the **Creator** button of a claw or gacha machine, the **Creator** tab of its Settings, or `/blockpopscreator`.
> Make collections for your world from your own skins, box textures, logos and Blockbench models. Give figures a rarity and skin variants, make plush collections for the gacha machine, and try the draw out without spending tokens.
> A collection stays a draft, saved as you work, until you publish it into the machines.
> A logo that is too big is scaled down to fit.

- **Haikyuu plushes**
> A new collection of 19 plushes in the gacha machine. Twelve of them are specials.

- **New Adventure Time specials**
> BMO, Prince Gumball, Marshall Lee, Huntress Wizard, Lemongrab (right-click it for Lemongrab 2), The Lich and Fern.

- **Link your Ko-fi without leaving the game**
> After the supporter button opens Ko-fi, a window asks for the email you paid with. Type it and the special plushes unlock about a minute later.
> `/blockpops supporter link` opens it again.

- **Servers can stop the favourite colour screen on joining**
> `/blockpops showcolorselectiononjoin false`. A player without a colour is then asked the first time they open a machine.

## Fixes:
- **Holographic figures show up with shader packs (Iris, Oculus)**
> While a shader pack is on, they are drawn without the holographic effect instead of being invisible.

- **A prize no longer disappears when your inventory is full**
> It drops at your feet.

- **Boxes**
> The lid no longer shuts again while the box is open.
> A box you place no longer flashes as an empty box.
> Shift-right-click on a closed box changes the figure's skin again, and no longer changes the pose of an empty box.

- **The Lich plush has its model in the inventory on newer Minecraft versions**

- **The game's notifications wait until you close a Block Pops menu**

**The server and every player must use Block Pops 2.1.0.**
